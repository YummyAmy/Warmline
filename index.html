Frequently Asked Questions

Is Warmline free?

Yes. Transparency: You have fifteen (15) open lines (Warmline) per browser, no credit card or signup. The paid tier (unlimited plus CSV import) is optional and comes later.

How is this different from Lemlist or Instantly?

Those are full outbound platforms at $30 to $99/mo. Warmline does one thing, gives you the first line, and does it in a human voice with a banned-robot-words list baked in. Think of it as meeting a CEO for the first time: what will you say and how will you say it? That's what Warmline is for.

Does it store my prospects?

No. Nothing you type is stored on our servers. The Warmline(s) are generated and handed straight back to you.

What makes the opening lines from Warmline different from AI opening lines?

Besides the fact that a human created it and it's conversational? There is a hard list of banned phrases plus a rule to "always" lead with the person, not with you.

Who made this?

Ame/Amy, a data scientist, interested in human, conversational outreaches and bland AI tones, and decided to build a tool for warm outreaches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Warmline free?

Yes. Transparency: You have fifteen (15) open lines (Warmline) per browser, no credit card or signup. The paid tier (unlimited plus CSV import) is optional and comes later.

How is this different from Lemlist or Instantly?

Those are full outbound platforms at $30 to 99/mo. Warmline does one thing, gives you the first line, and does it in a human voice with a banned-robot-words list baked in. Think of it as meeting a CEO for the first time: what you would say and how you will say it? that's Warmline.

Does it store my prospects?

No. Nothing you type is saved anywhere. The Warmline(s) are generated and handed straight back to you.

What makes the opening lines from Warmline different from AI opening lines?

Besides the fact that a human created it and it's conversational? There is a hard list of banned phrases plus a rule to "always" lead with the person, not with you.

Who made this?

Ame/Amy, a data scientist who got curious about conversational outreaches and bland AI tones, and wants to rebuild more overpriced tools.

Privacy Policy

Effective 6 August 2026  .  Last updated 6 August 2026

The short version, which is not the legal version but is honest: there is no account, no login and no tracking pixel. The prospect names, companies and details you type are sent to our AI provider to write your line and then they are gone. We do not write them to a database, we do not sell them, and they are not used to train any model. We do store an email address if you hand us one, a note if you leave one, and a per-day count that keeps the free tier alive. Everything below explains that in full.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Warmline is operated by data according to me ("Warmline", "we", "us", "our") at warmline.dataaccordingtome.com.

For personal data about you, the visitor, we are the controller. For personal data about the people you write about, you are the controller and we act as your processor. That split matters, and it is set out properly in our Data Processing Addendum.

Contact for all privacy matters: amy@dataaccordingtome.com.

3. What we collect, and what we do not

3.1 Information you give us directly

  • Prospect input: the name, company or newsletter, researched detail and outreach reason you type into the tool, plus the tone you pick.
  • Email address, only if you type one into the newsletter box or into the "tell me when it is back" box that appears when a daily cap is reached.
  • A note and an optional display name, only if you use the notes box.
  • Correspondence, if you email us. We keep the thread so we can answer you.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, used to enforce a per-visitor daily limit. It is used to build a rate-limit key that expires after 48 hours.
  • Standard request data held by our hosting provider: timestamp, requested path, response status, user agent, approximate region. These are ordinary web server logs and we do not join them to anything else.
  • Local storage in your own browser, holding the number of free lines you have used. This never leaves your device.

3.3 What we do not collect

  • We do not run advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or third party analytics that follow you between sites.
  • We do not ask for or store passwords, because there are no accounts.
  • We do not store payment card details. If we introduce paid plans, a payment processor handles the card and we never receive the full number.
  • We do not buy contact data, enrich the names you type, or look anybody up.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Prospect data: the important part

When you type a person's name into Warmline, you are handing us personal data about somebody who is not in the room. We treat that seriously, and the design reflects it: that data is passed through, not kept.

Here is exactly what happens when you press "Write the line":

  • Your browser posts the prospect fields, the tone you selected and your outreach reason to our own endpoint at /api/write. Nothing else is sent: the tone wording and the banned-phrase list live on the server, so the browser cannot influence either.
  • Each field is length-capped so nothing oversized gets forwarded.
  • The endpoint checks two counters (yours for the day, and the site's for the day) to decide whether to proceed. Those counters are keyed on your IP address and the date. They hold no prospect data.
  • If it proceeds, the endpoint builds a prompt containing your fields and sends it to our AI model provider over an encrypted connection.
  • The provider returns a line. We tidy the punctuation, add one to a global counter of lines written, and return the line to your browser.
  • At no point is the prospect name, company, detail, outreach reason or the generated line written to any database, log or file that we control. When the request ends, it is gone from our side.

Our AI model provider processes the prompt under its own commercial terms, which include a commitment not to use business API inputs or outputs to train its models. It retains inputs only briefly for abuse detection and safety purposes. See section 7.

Two honest limits on that promise. First, if a request errors, the error status and a truncated provider message may be written to our hosting provider's function logs so we can debug. We do not deliberately log your prospect fields, but you should not rely on a technical guarantee that nothing ever appears in a log line. Second, our hosting provider necessarily sees the request in transit as the operator of the server.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Warmline sets no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

We use one item of browser local storage, named opener_used, holding a single number: how many free lines you have generated on that browser. It is strictly necessary to operate the free tier, it stays on your device, and it is never transmitted to us. Clearing your site data removes it.

Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary cookie for security or load balancing.

We do keep first-party product analytics, and they are deliberately minimal. They are plain counters held in our own storage: how many times the page was loaded, how many lines were generated, how many people pressed the example button or shared a line, and which of the four tones were chosen. There is no third party involved, no cookie, no device or browser fingerprint, and nothing that identifies you or follows you to another website. The counters are totals only, so no individual visit can be picked out of them.

Pages on this site link to third party sites (Substack, Gumroad, GitHub, LinkedIn, Upwork, Buy Me a Coffee). Those sites set their own cookies once you arrive. We do not control them.

8. International transfers

We operate from, and our providers are principally based in, the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, your data will be transferred outside your country.

Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable, or the EU-US and UK-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified. We also apply the practical safeguards described in this policy: minimal collection, no retention of prospect data, and encryption in transit.

You can ask us for information about the transfer mechanism used for a particular provider by emailing us.

9. How long we keep things

  • Prospect input and generated lines: not retained. They exist for the duration of the request.
  • Rate-limit keys containing your IP: automatically expire 48 hours after creation.
  • The site-wide count of lines written: kept indefinitely. It is a single integer with nothing personal attached.
  • Newsletter email addresses: kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, then removed.
  • Notes: kept until you ask for removal. Notes are held in a pending list and reviewed before any of them are displayed.
  • Email correspondence: kept for up to 24 months so we have a record of what was asked and answered.
  • Hosting logs: per our hosting provider's standard retention, typically around 30 days.

10. Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. API credentials live in server-side environment variables and are never exposed to the browser, which is the specific reason generation runs through our own endpoint rather than calling a model provider from the page. Input fields are length-capped, submitted notes are stripped of markup and escaped on render, and rate limits are enforced server-side where they cannot be bypassed by clearing browser storage.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where the law requires it, and we will notify you directly without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights.

11. Your rights (GDPR and UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to limited exceptions.
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Port your data to another controller in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing at any time, absolutely.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Complain to a supervisory authority (see section 19).

To exercise any of these, email amy@dataaccordingtome.com. We will respond within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests, and we will tell you if we need the extension. We do not charge a fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

Because we hold so little, most requests are quick. If you only ever generated lines and never gave us an email address or a note, we most likely hold nothing about you beyond an expired rate-limit key and a hosting log entry.

12. Your rights (California)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

In the preceding twelve months we have collected the following statutory categories: identifiers (IP address, and email address if you gave one) and internet or network activity information (server log data). We collect these for the business purposes described in section 5 and disclose them only to the service providers listed in section 7. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we ask you not to submit any.

To exercise a right, email amy@dataaccordingtome.com with "CCPA request" in the subject. We will verify your request by matching the information you supply against what we hold. An authorised agent may submit on your behalf with written proof of authority.

13. Other US state privacy rights

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have comparable rights to access, correct, delete and port their data, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling. We do not engage in targeted advertising, sale or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Use the same contact route and we will honour your request.

Where your state provides an appeal process for a refused request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. We will respond to the appeal within the period your state's law requires and will tell you how to contact your Attorney General if you remain unsatisfied.

14. If you are the person somebody wrote about

If you believe someone entered your details into Warmline, here is the position. We do not hold them. We do not maintain any index, list or profile of people who have been written about, so there is nothing for us to search, disclose or delete on our side.

The controller of that data is the person who typed it in and who sent you the message. Your access, objection and erasure rights are exercisable against them, and their contact details should be in the message you received.

If you email us anyway, we will confirm the above in writing so you have a record, and we will assist the sender in responding to you as our Data Processing Addendum requires. If you believe someone is using Warmline abusively, tell us at amy@dataaccordingtome.com and we will act on it.

15. Children

Warmline is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it promptly. Do not enter a child's personal data into the tool.

16. Automated decision making

The tool generates text automatically, which is its purpose. We do not carry out automated decision making that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you, and we do not profile you. Rate limiting is a simple counter, not a decision about you as a person.

17. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track you across sites, so a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal does not change what we do. We honour those signals as an opt-out of sale and sharing by default, because we never sell or share in the first place.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially reduces your privacy protection, we will make a reasonable effort to flag it on the site, and by email if you are subscribed, before it takes effect.
